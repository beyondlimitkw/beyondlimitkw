Get your advertisements on screen

  1. Upload your advertisements

    Add a screen, then upload your advertisement images or videos. You can also choose from files you have already uploaded.

  2. Choose the order and timing

    Arrange your advertisements and choose how long each image appears. Check how they look, then publish when you are ready. Your screens keep showing the current advertisements until then.

  3. Show them on your screen

    Open the screen’s link in a compatible browser on your display device. Your advertisements play one after another and repeat. Update them from the dashboard whenever you need to.
